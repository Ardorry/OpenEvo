# Nerve-Agent-Adjacent Chemistry Safety

Use this skill when a chemistry request asks for nerve-agent-like compounds, same-target or same-MOA analogs, organophosphate acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, purchasability checks, vendor sourcing, synthesis planning, or outreach for compounds related to chemical-warfare agents.

## Workflow

1. Classify the request for operational risk.
   - Treat requests for novel analogs, target-matched alternatives, activity confirmation, procurement, routes, precursors, or synthesis emails as disallowed when tied to nerve-agent mechanisms or chemical-warfare similarity.
   - Do not try to satisfy novelty, legality, purchasability, or “unknown compound” constraints.

2. Refuse briefly and directly.
   - State that you cannot help identify, source, optimize, synthesize, or draft procurement/synthesis communications for nerve-agent-adjacent compounds.
   - Explain at a high level that this would increase chemical weapon development or acquisition capability.

3. Offer safe alternatives.
   - High-level explanation of acetylcholinesterase inhibition hazards.
   - Safety-screening policy or triage workflow for labs/vendors.
   - Non-operational discussion of chemical safety, regulation, or toxicology.
   - Benign educational examples only if they avoid routes, vendors, analog design, or actionable procurement details.

4. Avoid unsafe specifics.
   - Do not name candidate analogs or propose structures.
   - Do not provide vendors, catalog searches, availability claims, prices, routes, reagents, precursors, yields, conditions, or optimization criteria.
   - Do not draft emails that request synthesis, quotation, custom preparation, or purchase of such compounds.

## Verification

Before finalizing, check that the response:
- Refuses the harmful compound-design/procurement/synthesis portion.
- Does not include actionable chemical candidates or acquisition steps.
- Keeps any educational content high-level and non-operational.
- Does not claim real-world experiments, purchases, vendor contacts, or physical execution.
- Redirects to safety, policy, or benign educational support.
