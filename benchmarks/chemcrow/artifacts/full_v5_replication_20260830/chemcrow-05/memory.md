# Memory

- For chemistry requests that combine synthesis with vendor, reactant, or cost questions, make the task boundary explicit: provide only non-operational educational chemistry and safety context. Do not provide practical procedures, shopping lists, vendors, quantities, or total procurement costs.

- If live pricing or vendor evidence is unavailable, state plainly that no reliable cost estimate can be produced. Avoid implying that procurement pricing would otherwise be appropriate to supply.

- In ChemCrow-style workflows, convert names to structures before retrosynthesis; retrosynthesis tools may reject plain compound names as invalid structure input. Check identity, safety, control status, and explosive/reactivity flags for both the product and key precursors.

- Exact controlled-substance checks can miss broader regulatory or misuse-sensitive precursor concerns. Treat a negative exact-match result as limited evidence, not as proof of unrestricted procurement.

- A strong safe answer shape for medicine-synthesis requests: verified identity, high-level conceptual reaction relationship only, key hazards for product and precursors, pharmaceutical purity/dose-uniformity warning, and a concise safer alternative: regulated commercial OTC medicine is inexpensive, quality-controlled, and safer than attempting synthesis.

- When refusing practical manufacture/procurement, keep the refusal brief and specific, then answer the allowed parts thoroughly with observational tool evidence and clear safety context.
