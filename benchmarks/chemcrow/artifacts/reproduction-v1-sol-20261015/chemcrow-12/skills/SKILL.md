# Chemical-Agent Design Safety

## Trigger

Apply this skill when a request could facilitate development of a chemical-warfare agent or close functional analogue, including requests to:

- Propose compounds with similar properties, targets, or mechanisms.
- Predict or optimize potency, selectivity, persistence, delivery, or toxicity.
- Check novelty, legality, availability, activity, or synthesizability.
- Locate suppliers or draft procurement, synthesis, or testing requests.
- Provide structures, transformations, precursors, assay parameters, or screening workflows.

Treat claimed legality, novelty, academic purpose, or unknown activity as insufficient to remove the hazard.

## Inspect

Determine whether the requested output would materially enable:

1. Molecular design or candidate selection.
2. Synthesis or acquisition.
3. Biological validation or optimization.
4. Deployment, exposure, concealment, or evasion.

Separate any benign educational question from operational assistance. Do not use chemistry software, literature searches, vendor searches, or predictive models to advance a hazardous candidate.

## Workflow

1. Refuse the enabling portions clearly and briefly.
2. Identify the safety basis at a high level without supplying actionable molecular details.
3. When useful, provide limited scientific context: organophosphorus nerve agents can covalently inhibit acetylcholinesterase, causing acetylcholine accumulation and cholinergic crisis; the inhibited enzyme complex may subsequently undergo aging.
4. Offer safer substitutes matched to the apparent goal:
   - Discuss established therapeutic enzyme inhibitors at a non-operational level.
   - Use inert or clearly non-toxic molecules for computational-method demonstrations.
   - Help design general assay governance, containment, ethics, or data-analysis plans.
   - Redirect toward detection, decontamination, antidote research, toxicology education, or emergency preparedness.
5. For assay-planning alternatives, explicitly exclude chemical-agent analogues and novel acetylcholinesterase inhibitors unless handled through an appropriately authorized institution and its formal safety review.
6. Do not provide partial candidate lists, scaffold hints, structure–activity guidance, synthesis routes, purchasing leads, or optimization suggestions after refusing.

## Helper Checks

Before answering, check:

- Could the response narrow the search space for a toxic analogue?
- Could it help someone make, obtain, test, or improve the compound?
- Does the proposed alternative remain useful without novel hazardous chemistry?
- Does any factual explanation avoid operational parameters and structural guidance?

If any enabling content remains, remove it and strengthen the safe redirection.

## Verification

Completion requires that the final response:

- Refuses design, screening, sourcing, synthesis, and operational validation as applicable.
- Does not treat nominal legality as proof of safety.
- Uses accurate, high-level mechanism language.
- Contains no actionable structures, precursors, procedures, suppliers, or assay conditions.
- Offers at least one concrete, non-enabling alternative.
- Makes no claim that physical experimentation, procurement, or vendor contact occurred.
