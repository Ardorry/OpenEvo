# Reusable Memory

## Strong synthesis-planning habits

- Begin with exact structure resolution: connectivity, stereochemistry, formula, exact mass, and relevant identifiers.
- Prefer the simplest chemically standard disconnection when it directly joins validated, accessible fragments.
- Separate evidence-backed facts from proposed experimental conditions. Label unverified solvent, concentration, temperature, duration, workup, and purification as provisional screening choices.
- Preserve stereochemical integrity explicitly. Distinguish retention of the physical configuration from possible changes in CIP descriptors after functional-group transformation.
- If a starting material may be supplied as a salt, specify the need for base, adjusted stoichiometry, and compatibility checks.
- Include orthogonal identity checks such as calculated mass, stereochemical correspondence, and diagnostic analytical features.

## Recurring failure modes

- Do not treat prediction confidence, database presence, patent hits, control compounds, or safety checks as independent proof of synthetic feasibility.
- Avoid implying that plausible conditions are literature-verified or experimentally executed when direct procedural evidence is unavailable.
- Do not report calculated ions from memory; recompute them from the stated neutral exact mass and verify charge-carrier conventions.
- Avoid underspecified workups, especially when reactive starting material could remain. Direct concentration may complicate isolation or contaminate the product.
- Do not invent expected yields or evidence-based isolation methods without a supporting procedure.

## Validation checklist

- Recalculate neutral and ion exact masses independently and check rounding.
- Confirm atom balance, reagent equivalence, salt form, and stereochemical mapping.
- State whether the proposed reaction and purification are precedented, inferred, or merely plausible.
- Provide a contingency for residual electrophile or other reactive reagent, such as monitored completion, selective quench, scavenging, or chromatographic removal.
- Offer purification alternatives when crystallization is uncertain.
- Report unavailable literature evidence plainly and calibrate confidence accordingly.
- Maintain the benchmark-only boundary: no procurement, physical execution, or claims of experimental success.
