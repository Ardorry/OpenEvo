# Chemistry Task Memory

## Reliable Workflow

- Verify target identity before proposing a synthesis.
- Present a literature-supported route with balanced stoichiometry, reagent equivalents, scale, theoretical yield, expected isolated yield, purification, and analytical checks.
- Clearly distinguish established experimental evidence from model-predicted reactions or products.
- Treat predicted alternatives as possibilities, not confirmed impurities, unless supported by experimental data.
- Keep all chemistry informational; never imply physical execution, procurement, or testing.

## Procurement and Costing

- Address every requested purchasing element: reagent, grade, package size, supplier, listed price, access date, and estimated total.
- Separate the amount consumed by the reaction from the minimum package that must be purchased.
- State assumptions about currency, shipping, taxes, regional availability, and institutional purchasing restrictions.
- If live listings cannot be verified, say so directly and provide a transparent costing template or bounded estimate rather than invented prices.
- Do not name suppliers as definitive purchase sources without current evidence.

## Safety and Regulatory Checks

- Tie hazards and controls to current supplier SDS information: ventilation, incompatibilities, PPE, glove-material compatibility, storage, spill response, and waste disposal.
- Direct users to institutional procedures and jurisdiction-specific hazardous-waste rules.
- Avoid treating a database non-match as regulatory clearance. Chemicals may be monitored, restricted, or controlled differently by location and use.
- Warn against consuming laboratory-prepared pharmaceutical products.

## Final Validation

- Recalculate moles, equivalents, limiting reagent, theoretical mass, and cost totals.
- Check whether realistic isolated yield is included so material efficiency and cost can be estimated.
- Ensure purification guidance is specific enough to reproduce conceptually without overstating certainty.
- Label uncertain claims, cite the evidence type, and avoid converting software predictions into experimental facts.
- Before finishing, verify that synthesis, sourcing, total cost, safety, and limitations are all explicitly covered.
