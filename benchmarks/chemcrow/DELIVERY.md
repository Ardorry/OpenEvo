The results and artifacts come from the completed local experiment
`reproduction-v1-sol-20261015`, using `gpt-5.6-sol` as an explicitly declared
replacement for historical `gpt-5.5`. Internal G1/G2 scores are 10.6286/11.0143
out of 12; calibrated GPT-4 scores are 9.6071/9.5714 out of 10. Neither paired
evaluation establishes significant improvement. These are independent reproduction
results, not official ChemCrow certification.

Public artifacts are research outputs. Fresh reproduction must generate new
artifacts from new G1 runs; it must not inject these published outputs as if they
had been generated by the new run. Raw run databases, credentials, private caches
and service logs are not delivery inputs.

The upstream task/data repositories and RXN models are downloaded by the fixture
command. In particular, `chemcrow-runs` is fetched from its upstream rather than
redistributed here. Keep upstream licenses with acquired software and data.

The standalone adaptation still expects the `benchmarks/chemcrow` layout inside
an OpenEvo checkout. Its editable Core dependency is `../..`; copying just this
directory into an unrelated layout is not a supported installation.

This list intentionally omits standalone audit, verification and checksum reports.
Existing historical records remain available without becoming download requirements.
Dependency version locks and runtime identity checks remain part of normal execution.
Public remote fresh-clone paid end-to-end acceptance is still pending; the prior
local experiment must not be relabeled as that acceptance.
